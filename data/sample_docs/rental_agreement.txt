Landlord: Sunita Devi Agarwal, daughter of Late Shri Ram Prasad Agarwal, residing at 12 Vasant Vihar, New Delhi 110057, India (hereinafter referred to as "Landlord" or "Owner").

1. PROPERTY DESCRIPTION

1.1 The Landlord hereby agrees to let and the Tenant hereby agrees to take on rent the following property:

Address: Flat No. 304, Third Floor, Green Valley Apartments, Plot No. 56, Sector 22, Dwarka, New Delhi 110077, India.

Description: A two-bedroom residential apartment measuring approximately 1,200 square feet of carpet area, comprising two bedrooms, one living room, one kitchen, two bathrooms, one balcony, and one designated car parking space in the basement (Parking Slot B-34).

1.2 The property is let out in its present condition. The Tenant acknowledges having inspected the property and found it to be in satisfactory condition for residential use.

2. TERM OF LEASE

2.1 The lease shall commence on April 1, 2024 ("Commencement Date") and shall continue for a period of eleven (11) months, expiring on February 28, 2025 ("Expiry Date"), unless renewed or terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 Renewal: The Tenant may request renewal of this lease by providing written notice to the Landlord at least thirty (30) days before the Expiry Date. Renewal shall be subject to mutual agreement on revised terms, including a rent escalation of up to ten percent (10%) of the prevailing rent.

2.3 Lock-in Period: There shall be a lock-in period of six (6) months from the Commencement Date during which neither Party may terminate this Agreement without paying a penalty equivalent to two (2) months' rent to the other Party.

3. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 Monthly Rent: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of INR 35,000 (Indian Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand) to the Landlord.

3.2 Payment Due Date: Rent is due and payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. The first month's rent shall be paid on or before the Commencement Date.

3.3 Mode of Payment: Rent shall be paid via bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS/UPI) to the Landlord's designated bank account:
   - Bank: State Bank of India
   - Account Name: Sunita Devi Agarwal
   - Account Number: XXXX-XXXX-4567
   - IFSC Code: SBIN00XXXXX

3.4 Late Payment Penalty: If the rent is not received by the 10th day of the month, a late payment fee of INR 200 (Indian Rupees Two Hundred) per day of delay shall be applicable, up to a maximum of INR 3,000 (Indian Rupees Three Thousand) per month.

3.5 Rent Receipts: The Landlord shall provide monthly rent receipts to the Tenant within seven (7) days of receiving the rent payment.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of INR 1,75,000 (Indian Rupees One Lakh Seventy-Five Thousand), equivalent to five (5) months' rent, at the time of signing this Agreement.

4.2 The security deposit shall be refunded to the Tenant within thirty (30) days of vacating the property, after deducting:
(a) Any unpaid rent or utility charges;
(b) Cost of repairs for damages beyond normal wear and tear;
(c) Any other amounts owed by the Tenant under this Agreement.

4.3 The security deposit shall not bear any interest.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

5.1 Landlord's Responsibility: The Landlord shall be responsible for:
(a) Major structural repairs (roof, walls, plumbing pipes, electrical wiring);
(b) Repairs to pre-existing fixtures and fittings;
(c) Payment of property tax and building insurance.

5.2 Tenant's Responsibility: The Tenant shall be responsible for:
(a) Day-to-day maintenance and minor repairs (faucet washers, light bulbs, door handles);
(b) Monthly maintenance charges to the Resident Welfare Association (RWA), currently INR 4,500 per month;
(c) All utility bills including electricity, water, gas, internet, and DTH/cable TV;
(d) Keeping the premises clean and in good condition.

5.3 Any repair costing more than INR 5,000 shall require prior written approval from the Landlord. If the Landlord fails to carry out necessary major repairs within fifteen (15) days of written notice, the Tenant may carry out such repairs and deduct the reasonable cost from the next month's rent.

6. PERMITTED USE AND RESTRICTIONS

6.1 The property shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant and the Tenant's immediate family members.

6.2 The Tenant shall NOT:
(a) Sublet, assign, or transfer the property or any part thereof to any third party without the prior written consent of the Landlord;
(b) Use the property for any commercial, illegal, or immoral purpose;
(c) Make any structural alterations or modifications without the Landlord's prior written consent;
(d) Keep any pets on the premises without the Landlord's written permission;
(e) Cause any nuisance or disturbance to neighboring residents;
(f) Store any hazardous, inflammable, or explosive materials on the premises.

6.3 The Tenant may install temporary fixtures (curtains, shelves, air conditioning units) at their own cost. Such fixtures may be removed at the time of vacating, provided the Tenant restores the property to its original condition.

7. TERMINATION

7.1 Termination by Landlord: The Landlord may terminate this Agreement by providing sixty (60) days written notice to the Tenant, subject to the lock-in period specified in Section 2.3.

7.2 Termination by Tenant: The Tenant may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days written notice to the Landlord, subject to the lock-in period specified in Section 2.3.

7.3 Immediate Termination: The Landlord may terminate this Agreement immediately without notice if:
(a) The Tenant fails to pay rent for two (2) consecutive months;
(b) The Tenant uses the property for illegal purposes;
(c) The Tenant causes substantial damage to the property;
(d) The Tenant violates any material term of this Agreement after receiving a written warning.

7.4 Upon termination, the Tenant shall vacate the property and hand over all keys, access cards, and parking passes to the Landlord. The property shall be returned in the same condition as received, subject to normal wear and tear.

8. LANDLORD'S RIGHT OF INSPECTION

8.1 The Landlord or the Landlord's authorized representative may inspect the property once every three (3) months, provided at least forty-eight (48) hours' prior written notice is given to the Tenant.

8.2 In case of emergency (fire, flooding, gas leak, or structural danger), the Landlord may enter the property without prior notice.

9. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

9.1 The Landlord shall not be liable for any loss, damage, or injury suffered by the Tenant or the Tenant's guests arising from the use of the property, except where caused by the Landlord's gross negligence.

9.2 The Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord against any claims, damages, or expenses arising from the Tenant's use of the property or any breach of this Agreement.

9.3 The Tenant is advised to obtain renter's insurance to cover personal belongings and liability.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India and the Delhi Rent Control Act, as applicable.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through mediation. If mediation fails within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be referred to the courts of competent jurisdiction in New Delhi, India.

11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement, along with any annexures, constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties.

11.2 Amendment: Any amendment to this Agreement shall be in writing and signed by both Parties.

11.3 Severability: The invalidity of any provision shall not affect the remaining provisions of this Agreement.

11.4 Notices: All notices shall be given in writing via registered post or email:
   - Landlord: sunita.agarwal@email.com
   - Tenant: amit.mehta@email.com

11.5 Force Majeure: Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations due to causes beyond their reasonable control, including natural disasters, government actions, or pandemics.
